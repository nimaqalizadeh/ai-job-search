# Behavioral Profile

## Status

No behavioral assessment or preference profile is configured.

## Evaluation Rule

- Do not infer personality traits from job titles, technologies, or CV wording.
- Do not claim behavioral strengths or growth areas in applications without direct evidence.
- When a job evaluation requires a behavioral/culture score, use a neutral score of 50 with low confidence and state that no preference profile is configured.
- Evaluate concrete evidence such as ownership, delivery, and cross-functional responsibilities under experience match, not as personality claims.

## Application Use

- **Cover letters:** Use only documented examples of ownership and delivery; avoid unsupported personality adjectives.
- **CV:** Emphasize verified responsibilities, technologies, scope, and outcomes.
- **Interviews:** Use the STAR candidates in `07-interview-prep.md`; do not invent behavioral claims.
